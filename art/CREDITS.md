# Flower and leaf artwork

All images in this folder are cut out from botanical illustrations that are in the
public domain (the artists died more than 100 years ago). The scans come from
Wikimedia Commons. `tools/make_art.py` rebuilds every file from the original scans.

## Product photos

`nest-board.webp` (bird's nests on a wooden board) and `dates.webp` (dried red dates)
are cut out of product photos supplied by the project owner with `tools/cut_photo.py`.
They are **not** public domain and are not covered by the statement above.
